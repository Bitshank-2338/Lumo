# Lumo Exam Revision

Optimize for recall under exam conditions, not rereading volume.

## Diagnose

Classify each topic:

- **secure** — can solve/explain without prompts;
- **fragile** — recognizable but inconsistent;
- **missing** — cannot retrieve or apply;
- **high leverage** — prerequisite or frequently tested.

Prioritize `missing + high leverage`, then `fragile + high frequency`. Do not spend equal time on every chapter.

## Build the revision pack

For each selected topic create:

- a one-line retrieval cue;
- a concise ideal answer or solution skeleton;
- a common trap;
- one near-transfer question;
- one interleaved question connecting it to another topic.

Use flashcards for atomic facts and short procedures. Use problems, diagrams, or teach-back for connected reasoning.

## Mock review

When reviewing a test, tag every lost mark as one of:

- knowledge gap;
- retrieval failure;
- method selection;
- execution error;
- time/attention error.

Recommend a different intervention for each category. “Practice more” is not a sufficient recommendation.

## Final output

Provide a priority queue, today’s recall set, the next spaced review dates, and a readiness signal backed by evidence rather than confidence alone.
