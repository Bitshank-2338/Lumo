# Lumo Governance

Lumo currently uses a maintainer-led governance model suitable for an early-stage project.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone who submits issues, code, skills, designs, documentation, testing, or review feedback in line with the Code of Conduct.

### Maintainers

Maintainers triage issues, set roadmap priorities, review and merge contributions, manage releases and repository settings, and respond to conduct and security reports. The current repository owner and lead maintainer is [@Bitshank-2338](https://github.com/Bitshank-2338).

## Decision making

Decisions are guided by learner value, safety, privacy, maintainability, accessibility, compatibility, test evidence, and roadmap fit. Maintainers seek contributor input on substantial changes, but the lead maintainer makes the final decision when consensus cannot be reached.

Substantial architectural, data-model, authentication, licensing, or product-scope changes should begin as an issue before implementation. Decisions and important tradeoffs should be recorded publicly when they are not security-sensitive.

## Contribution acceptance

A contribution may be accepted when it is correctly scoped, licensed, tested, documented, secure, maintainable, and aligned with Lumo’s direction. It may be declined or postponed even when technically sound if it creates excessive maintenance cost, duplicates another direction, lacks safe defaults, or is outside the current roadmap.

## Maintainer growth

Consistent contributors may be invited to take on triage or review responsibilities. Maintainer access is granted gradually based on sustained, constructive participation, technical judgment, reliability, and adherence to security and community standards.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes are proposed through a pull request and should explain the motivation and community impact. The lead maintainer approves changes to this document.
