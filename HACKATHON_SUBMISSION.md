# Lumo — Hackathon Submission Kit

## One-line pitch

Lumo is an agent-native learning workspace that turns personal material, goals, and AI tools into one continuous journey from curiosity to mastery to creation.

## Problem statement

Modern learners have access to more information and AI tools than ever, yet their actual learning workflow remains fragmented. Notes live in documents, course material lives in folders, AI conversations disappear into chat history, research happens in separate browser tabs, progress is tracked manually, and final deliverables are written in another application. Generic AI assistants optimize for answering the current prompt; they do not reliably manage a learner's long-term direction, source material, mastery, memory, specialized workflows, and final output together.

This fragmentation creates four problems:

1. **Context loss:** each new tool or conversation starts without the full learning history.
2. **Answer dependence:** instant answers can replace active understanding and practice.
3. **No path from knowledge to outcome:** research, study, building, and writing remain disconnected.
4. **Tool overload:** powerful agents, models, and services require technical coordination that most learners should not have to manage.

## Solution

Lumo provides one learning system where a user can:

- Ground AI in their own documents and knowledge collections.
- Turn an ambition into a measurable study or project plan.
- Learn through explanation, Socratic questioning, quizzes, and spaced mastery.
- Conduct deep, source-aware research instead of receiving untraceable summaries.
- Maintain memory and reusable personas across learning sessions.
- Bring specialized coding agents, MCP services, and CLI tools into the same workflow.
- Convert learning into notebooks, books, diagrams, research reports, applications, and other real deliverables.

The product is designed around direction rather than isolated answers: **learn deeply, then move toward a concrete outcome.**

## Target users

### Primary

- University and college students.
- Self-directed technical learners.
- Students preparing for exams, projects, internships, scholarships, or higher education.

### Secondary

- Researchers organizing papers and evidence.
- Educators creating guided learning experiences.
- Career switchers building structured learning paths and portfolios.
- Builders who learn by working alongside coding agents.

## Why existing tools are not enough

- Traditional learning-management systems organize courses but are not adaptive personal tutors.
- Generic chatbots answer questions but do not provide a complete mastery and creation workflow.
- Note-taking applications store information but do not actively teach or coordinate agents.
- Research assistants gather sources but usually stop before practice, building, and final production.
- Agent platforms are powerful but are rarely designed around learning safety, progression, and reflection.

Lumo connects all of these layers through one learner-centered interface.

## Core product pillars

### 1. Understand

Adaptive chat, grounded explanations, Socratic Coach, personas, file previews, visualizations, and animated mathematical explanations.

### 2. Plan

Study Planner, Opportunity Fit, goals, milestones, and Mastery Path progression.

### 3. Research

Deep Research, Source Research, web and paper search, editable research outlines, evidence inspection, and citations.

### 4. Practise

Quiz generation, Question Bank, bookmarked and incorrect-question review, exam revision, and spaced mastery.

### 5. Build

Connected local agents, Partners, MCP services, CLI applications, and tool-enabled conversations.

### 6. Create

Co-Writer, notebooks, Books, concept visualizations, image/video capabilities, and application coaching.

### 7. Remember

Long-term memory, reusable knowledge bases, session history, notebooks, personas, and saved learning progress.

## Key differentiators

- A complete loop from personal context to learning to a finished outcome.
- Agent-native architecture without making the learner manage agent complexity.
- Source-grounded research alongside mastery-based practice.
- Persistent knowledge and memory across multiple learning modes.
- First-party skills that behave like reusable learning playbooks.
- Connected coding agents for learning-by-building workflows.
- A customizable, open-source foundation with transparent attribution.

## Example end-to-end use case

A learner preparing for an AI research internship uploads a résumé, opportunity description, papers, and course notes. Lumo evaluates opportunity fit, identifies gaps, creates a six-week plan, teaches difficult concepts through Socratic dialogue and visualization, produces quizzes, conducts source-grounded research, asks a coding agent to help build a portfolio prototype, saves important results into notebooks, drafts the proposal and application in Co-Writer, and generates a Book documenting the complete journey.

## Product-demo sequence

Aim for a three-to-four-minute recording.

1. **Public website:** show the Lumo identity, product promise, Docs, and Lumo Hub.
2. **Home:** show the launchpad and explain that learning begins with a goal, not a menu of disconnected tools.
3. **Knowledge Center:** show how personal documents become reusable context. Use a non-sensitive sample document during the public recording.
4. **Adaptive chat:** ask Lumo to explain a concept using the selected knowledge source.
5. **Skills:** show the seven first-party Lumo playbooks.
6. **Deep Research:** demonstrate an editable research plan and evidence-aware output.
7. **Quiz and Mastery Path:** show that Lumo tests understanding instead of only providing answers.
8. **My Agents:** show how a coding assistant can be consulted inside the learning workflow.
9. **Learning Space:** show notebooks, Question Bank, personas, MCP services, CLI apps, and history.
10. **Co-Writer:** show research or learning becoming a polished deliverable.
11. **Book:** finish with the generated Lumo launch book as the record of the complete journey.
12. **Closing:** return to the product website and repeat the promise: “Learn deeply. Move with direction.”

## Product video script

### Opening — 0:00–0:20

“Learning today is not limited by access to information. It is limited by fragmentation. Our documents are in one place, AI chats are in another, research happens across browser tabs, and the final work is created somewhere else. Every transition loses context.”

### Introduce Lumo — 0:20–0:40

“This is Lumo—an agent-native learning workspace built to connect that entire journey. Lumo does more than answer a question. It helps you understand, plan, research, practise, build, and create while keeping your direction and learning context intact.”

### Personal knowledge — 0:40–1:05

“A learner can bring notes, papers, course material, and project documents into the Knowledge Center. Lumo can then ground explanations and research in the material that actually matters to that learner.”

### Tutor and skills — 1:05–1:35

“From the Home workspace, Lumo can explain a difficult concept, create a realistic study plan, or guide the learner through questions instead of immediately giving away the answer. Its first-party skill library includes study planning, Socratic coaching, exam revision, source research, opportunity evaluation, application coaching, and concept visualization.”

### Research and mastery — 1:35–2:05

“For complex questions, Deep Research creates an editable plan, gathers evidence, and produces a structured result. But learning does not end with reading. Lumo can generate quizzes, collect questions, revisit mistakes, and build a mastery path with spaced review.”

“When learning turns into building, Lumo can connect specialized assistants such as Codex or Claude Code, as well as MCP services and command-line tools. The learner can organize results in notebooks, turn ideas into polished work with Co-Writer, and compile the complete journey into an interactive Book.”

### Closing — 2:40–3:00

“Lumo brings personal knowledge, adaptive tutoring, research, practice, agents, memory, and creation into one continuous system. It is designed for students, researchers, and builders who do not just want an answer—they want to reach an outcome. Lumo: learn deeply, move with direction.”

## Success metrics

- Time from uploaded material to a useful grounded learning plan.
- Percentage of learners completing mastery checkpoints.
- Improvement between first and repeated quiz attempts.
- Number of workflows completed without leaving the learning workspace.
- Percentage of research outputs with inspectable supporting sources.
- User-reported clarity about the next best learning action.

## Responsible product choices

- Private user knowledge and credentials remain outside the public source repository.
- Community skills are not presented as Lumo-owned work.
- Community marketplace publishing remains “coming soon” until verification and security controls are ready.
- Lumo preserves the original DeepTutor attribution and Apache License 2.0 notices.
- Model and search providers are user-configurable, making data flow an explicit deployment choice.
